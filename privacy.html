HPO Mail – Privacy policy

HPO Mail is used only by its owner, on their own computer, to manage their own email accounts.

  • Email data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the owner's own email features: reading, filing, labelling, searching and drafting.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.
  • HPO Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
